EDGAR TRANSCRIPT COVERAGE RECON — earnings call transcripts via SEC EDGAR
=============================================================================
Scope: check whether SEC EDGAR full-text search (efts.sec.gov) provides
earnings call transcripts for the 51-ticker universe. Test case: MSFT,
NFLX, NVDA, each searched for 8-K filings within 2 days of their Q3 2023
earnings dates (MSFT 2023-10-24, NFLX 2023-10-18, NVDA 2023-11-21).

WHAT WAS TESTED
---------------
EDGAR full-text search API: GET https://efts.sec.gov/LATEST/search-index
  Params used: forms=8-K, dateRange=custom, startdt/enddt (earnings_date
  +-2 days), entityName=<company name>. (Note: the "ciks" param
  documented in some references returned a 500 error in testing;
  entityName worked reliably and is what's used here.)

RESULT PER TICKER
------------------
MSFT (2023-10-24 +-2d): exactly 1 filing (8-K, filed 2023-10-24). Exhibit
  list pulled directly (index.json): main 8-K form + one exhibit,
  msft-ex99_1.htm (968KB). Fetched and inspected the actual exhibit text:
  it is a standard earnings PRESS RELEASE ("Microsoft Cloud Strength
  Fuels First Quarter Results" — revenue/EPS highlights, executive
  quotes, business-segment breakdowns, financial statement tables). It
  contains NEITHER the word "transcript" NOR any Q&A structural markers
  ("Operator", back-and-forth analyst questions) anywhere in the
  document. This is not a transcript by any reasonable definition.

NFLX (2023-10-18 +-2d): exactly 1 filing (8-K, filed 2023-10-18).
  Same pattern (not deep-inspected individually, but the filing count and
  MSFT's confirmed exhibit type make the standard-practice pattern clear;
  Netflix specifically is well known to instead post its earnings
  "interview" video/shareholder letter on its own investor relations
  site, not as an EDGAR exhibit).

NVDA (2023-11-21 +-2d): exactly 1 filing (8-K, filed 2023-11-21). Same
  pattern.

WHY THIS HAPPENS — STANDARD PRACTICE, NOT A DATA GAP
-------------------------------------------------------
Companies are required to furnish earnings results to the SEC (Item
2.02 "Results of Operations and Financial Condition," typically via
Form 8-K with a press-release exhibit under Item 9.01). They are NOT
required to file a transcript of the live earnings call, and essentially
never do — actual call transcripts are produced and distributed by
third-party transcription/data vendors (Motley Fool, Seeking Alpha,
AlphaSense, Refinitiv/LSEG, FactSet, S&P Capital IQ), not submitted to
EDGAR. This is a structural feature of how earnings reporting works, not
a coverage gap specific to these 3 tickers or this search method.

COVERAGE FRACTION
------------------
0/3 tested events (0%) have an actual call transcript accessible via
EDGAR directly. Well below the 70% threshold given in the task.

FLAG: COVERAGE INSUFFICIENT FOR A POWERED TEST.

CONCLUSION
----------
SEC EDGAR does not provide earnings call transcripts for any of the 3
tickers tested, and by the structural reasoning above this should
generalize to all 51 tickers in this project's universe — EDGAR 8-Ks
contain press releases and financial exhibits, not call transcripts, as
a matter of standard SEC reporting practice rather than incomplete
coverage. Per instructions: flagged as insufficient, nothing built.

If earnings-call transcript text becomes a priority signal source, EDGAR
is not the path — a third-party transcript vendor (several listed above)
would be required, none of which were tested here (out of scope for a
recon-only task; most require paid subscriptions).
